Our services

Locksmith services for Chandler homes, businesses and vehicles

Most locksmith calls come down to three things: getting back in when a key is lost, broken or locked inside; controlling who can open a door after a move, a staff change or a lost set of keys; and fixing or upgrading hardware that is worn out or easy to defeat.

Chandler’s summer heat and monsoon dust are hard on exterior locks and car remotes, so a grinding cylinder or a fob that only works up close is worth a call before it quits for good. Tell us what happened and where you are, and we will confirm the job and the price before any work starts.

Car key replacement & auto locksmith

Lost your only car key, snapped one in the ignition, or have a fob that only works when it is touching the dash? We handle car key replacement where your car is, anywhere in Chandler and across the Valley: transponder keys cut and programmed, key fobs and remotes replaced and paired, and spare keys made while we are there.

Lost every key? A new one can often be made from the vehicle’s VIN — here is how getting a key made from your VIN works. Have the year, make and model ready, and proof that the car is yours.

As a full automotive locksmith, we also open locked cars and trunks, repair and replace ignitions and car door locks, and handle high-security and V.A.T.S. keys.

Residential locksmith

Just bought a home in Chandler? Rekey the locks before you unpack — previous owners, builders and neighbors may still have keys. Rekeying keeps your hardware, makes every old key useless, and can set the whole house to one key.

We repair sticking and worn locks, replace deadbolts and door hardware, and install keypad and smart locks. Locked out? Our house lockout service gets you back inside.

We also cut duplicate house keys, rekey and replace mailbox, side-gate and pool-gate locks, and open locked home safes.

FAQ

Common locksmith questions

If yours is not here, call and ask — (602) 388-1201.

Q.01What locksmith services does Diamondback Lock & Key offer in Chandler?

Residential, commercial and automotive locksmith work, from our shop in downtown Chandler and across the Valley.

That includes car key replacement and key fob programming, home and business rekeying, lock repair and replacement, deadbolt and smart lock installation, master key systems, push bars and exit devices, access control, safe and file-cabinet lockouts, and 24/7 emergency lockout service.

Q.02Do you offer 24/7 emergency lockout service in Chandler?

Yes — emergency callouts run 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Whether you are locked out of your house, your car or your business, call (602) 388-1201 and we will tell you when a technician can reach you. Arrival time depends on where you are and how busy that day is.

Q.03Can you replace a car key if I have lost every key?

Yes. Losing every key does not stop us from making a new one.

Car key replacement starts with the year, make and model, and proof that the vehicle is yours. When no key is left, a new one can often be made from the vehicle’s VIN. We also duplicate spare keys and replace and program key fobs and remotes.

Q.04Do you program key fobs and transponder keys?

Yes — we cut and program transponder keys and replace and program key fobs and remotes.

Modern car keys carry a chip that has to be paired with the vehicle, so a cut key alone will not start the engine. Tell us the year, make and model when you call and we will confirm the job before we come out.

Q.05Should I rekey or replace my locks?

Rekey when the question is who has a key; replace when the lock itself is worn out or you want an upgrade.

Rekeying changes the pins inside your existing lock so every old key stops working — the usual choice after buying a home in Chandler, a roommate moving out or a lost key. Replacement makes sense for a lock that sticks, a damaged deadbolt, or a move to smart or high-security hardware.

Q.06What commercial locksmith services do you provide for Chandler businesses?

Commercial lock replacement and rekeying, master key systems, exit devices, door hardware and access control.

We install and service Grade 1 commercial levers, storeroom levers, mortise locks, push bars and panic hardware, door closers and keyless access control, and we open and rekey file cabinets and safes. With a master key system in place, an employee leaving means rekeying only the doors that person could open.

Q.07Do you install smart locks and keypad deadbolts?

Yes — keypad deadbolts and smart locks for homes, and keyless access control for businesses.

We fit the new lock to your existing door, match it to the rest of your keys where the lock has a key cylinder, and walk you through setting the codes before we leave.

Since 2009.

One month full warranty, and after that, a year warranty through the manufacturer, just for the parts.
